**QyranEye — fall detection in drone video for search and rescue**

QyranEye watches drone video and raises an alarm when a person falls and stays down, so rescuers check that spot
first instead of watching hours of footage. The final decision always stays with a person.

**Try the working prototype:** https://conrad-qyran.pages.dev
- "Examples": three videos already processed by the system (a real fall in the snow → alarm; two people lying down
  on purpose, filmed by a drone at 10 m → no false alarm).
- "Try it with your own video": upload a drone video up to 30 s, choose the camera view, and get the annotated video
  and the list of alarms in about 2–5 minutes. It runs on a free cloud server: the first visit may take ~30 s to start.

**Status:** working prototype tested on public drone datasets (Okutama-Action, NOMAD, MOD20) with no false alarms on
the clips checked; real falls filmed by our own drone (FIMI X8 SE, 5–20 m) are the next step.
